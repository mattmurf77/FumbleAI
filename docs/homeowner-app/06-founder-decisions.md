Homeowner app — my decisions

- 8. Minimum iOS version: Plan A
- 9. Household sharing (spouse or partner): Plan A
- 10. iPad support: Plan A
- 11. Which floor the app opens on: Discuss — Ground floor by default, user can decide in settings.
- 12. Swipe to change floors: Plan B — B at launch. Test A after launch.

PUSHBACK ON MY IDEA
- No automatic floor plans from Bright MLS or public listings: Agree
- Pantry and clothing inventory get no dedicated screens: Discuss — Don't agree. Users would want to log this detail (especially clothing) by housemate and by location in the house. Think: "Where did I store my winter clothes during the summer"?
- iPhone only, with data in the user's iCloud: Agree

MY ANSWERS
- Who is the target user? New Homebuyers but existing homebuyers would see value too.
- Exact dimensions or recognizable? Let's wait and see how well the public record data is first. Dimensions would help with planning out applicances and furniture. Users should be able to add exact measurements into the app: "I have 32 inches of width and 40 inches of depth for my stove/fridge." "My front door is 72 inches tall and 36 inches wide." are two such use cases in mind. This would also help tremendously with yard/ landscape planning.
- Is a Home History Report a headline feature? Yes. Don't need to build it now, but for the future
- Open to real estate agent partnerships later? In the future, yes. Real estate agent partnerships, contractor partnerships for work, retailers for appliances and furniture, delivery companies/grocery delivery for food.
- OK with AI features that upload home photos? Yes
