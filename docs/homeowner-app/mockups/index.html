Homeowner App Mockups

High-fidelity iPhone mockups (393×852 pt) for the merged plan v1. The first phone is clickable: change floors with the pills, switch among the seven views with the dropdown, tap a room to open its sheet, tap “+” to add, and tap the pencil for edit mode. Sample house: 14 Linden Ct, three levels plus the exterior, with housemates Matt and Sam. The mockups are dated Tuesday, Sep 29, 2026.

01Interactive canvas

Home canvasIt opens on Ground with the Plan view. Try Kitchen in To-Dos, Primary Bath in Future Projects, and Exterior in Budget.

How the canvas reads

One floor at a time. The dropdown changes only what is drawn on the rooms and what the room sheet lists. The walls stay the same in every view.

Plan: small-caps room name plus width × depth. To-Dos: a chip counts chores due within 7 days. A red inner edge means at least one is overdue. Future Projects: the tint steps at under $1k, $1k–$5k and $5k or more planned. A chip shows the total. Past Work: lifetime spend chip plus the month of the last work. Appliances, Electronics & Furniture: plan-symbol icons at each item’s pin, with a count in the corner. Inventory: item count and number of storage spots. Budget: planned vs. spent per room. Floor and property totals appear in the bottom strip.
  • Floors change with the pills only (decision #12). Swipe is tested after TestFlight.
  • Pinch to zoom and drag to pan inside the canvas. A room tap opens its sheet at half height. A “+” tap opens the add picker, which defaults to the active view.
  • Rooms with nothing in the current view fade their label to grey, so the rooms that matter stand out.
